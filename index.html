BEFORE YOU LOG IN

Quick answers.

Which version is supported?

Shardot is built for WoW Forever, Interface 16001. Check the CurseForge files to confirm compatibility with your client before downloading.

Does it count shards in the bank?

No. It counts shards in your character’s bags, including soul bags. Bank storage is excluded.

What about restrictions during combat?

When the client restricts resource values, the gold-to-red progression uses a fixed color for each position. Hiding at zero only applies when the value can be read.

Moving from Forever Resources?

Disable or remove Forever Resources before enabling Shardot to avoid duplicate trackers. Previous settings are not imported automatically.

Where can I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Leave a comment on CurseForge with your client version, class and steps to reproduce the issue. Include the Lua error if available.

DIMINISHING RETURNS TRACKER Explore DRPulse ↗

Read the color. Time your control.

Another addon of mine for WoW Forever PvP. Each icon tracks a crowd-control category on your target. Its segments show how long your next control will last.

  • 1 yellow segment: 50%

    Your next control in this category lasts half its normal duration.

  • 2 orange segments: 25%

    Your next control in this category lasts a quarter of its normal duration.

  • 3 red segments: immunity

    The target is immune to controls in this category until diminishing returns reset.

The number is the reset timer. For example, 4.2 means 4.2 seconds until controls in that category return to full duration.
